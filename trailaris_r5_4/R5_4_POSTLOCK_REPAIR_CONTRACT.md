# R5.4 Post-Lock Repair Contract

R5.4 remains the immutable locked Market Execution Engine control. The following are successor-research repairs only until they beat the lock under full-universe chronological evidence.

## 1. Brief-favourable-then-loss class
Do not presume every such loss is avoidable, but treat the class as a mandatory research failure investigation. Partition by asset, strategy, family, regime, session, catalyst state, cross-asset confirmation, liquidity, MFE, MAE and stop geometry. A trade that reaches small positive MFE and then stops must be tested for: weak thesis, late/early entry, false structural confirmation, missing catalyst/regime evidence, correlated factor crowding, poor stop placement, or unavoidable path randomness.

## 2. Protected flat class
The 425 flat outcomes in locked R5.4 are not to be deleted or converted by hindsight. Audit their MFE distribution and persistence by route/strategy/regime. Test causal alternatives: delayed break-even only when thesis strength remains high; partial conversion at empirically justified MFE thresholds; structure-based rather than fixed-R protection; volatility/regime-specific protection; and earlier scratch for weak follow-through. Promotion requires higher net captured P&L with no material increase in loss tail, drawdown or fresh-holdout regression.

## 3. SUPPLY_DEMAND_SWEEP_BOS repair
Current approximate sweep+BOS logic is insufficient as final institutional research. Successor variants must test native supply/demand zone provenance and freshness, higher-timeframe trend/strength, liquidity sweep quality, displacement magnitude, close location, retest/acceptance or rejection, session/liquidity state, catalyst context, cross-asset confirmation and risk/reward to the next opposing liquidity/zone. No trade solely because a 12-bar range was swept and one displacement candle printed.

## 4. MEAN_REVERSION_EXTREME repair
A z-score extreme in weak H1 trend is insufficient as a complete thesis. Successor variants must test volatility regime, structural range validity, exhaustion/rejection confirmation, return inside value, divergence/flow confirmation where available, catalyst avoidance/interpretation, session/liquidity, mean target quality, adverse-trend risk and payoff asymmetry. No trade solely because z30 exceeds +/-2.

## 5. Research depth
Every repair must remain asset-native under R5_4_INSTITUTIONAL_RESEARCH_CONSTITUTION.md. Research evidence must improve a thesis, not merely add factors. Factor interactions are admitted only when incremental causal value is demonstrated.

## 6. Testing
All candidate repairs use the full 34-route / 510-cell universe. Diagnostics may isolate a strategy to understand it, but final evidence must be recombined into the whole portfolio. Use rolling-origin or explicitly separated discovery/validation windows, preserve costs and risk rules, and never promote by retroactively deleting known losing trades.
