# Trailaris R5.4 Institutional Research Constitution

## Status
R5.4 is the locked Market Execution Engine control state. This constitution governs all post-lock refinement and successor research without changing the frozen R5.4 result.

## Core rule
Every system-level decision cycle must research the complete approved 34-route universe and complete 510 route-strategy-cell universe before portfolio opportunity ranking. Research is not a generic indicator pass. It is an asset-native Research -> Thesis -> Opportunity Ranking -> Execution -> Management -> Post-Trade Attribution process.

A route may legitimately execute zero trades only when no candidate survives its causal research, reliability, execution, risk and portfolio-allocation gates. Zero execution must never mean zero research or silent route removal.

## Portfolio research layers
For every route, as applicable, Trailaris must evaluate: macro/fundamental drivers; scheduled and unscheduled catalysts; cross-asset confirmation/divergence; positioning and flows; volatility and liquidity; higher-timeframe structure and supply/demand; session/open behaviour; execution conditions; scenario branches; invalidation conditions; expected holding period; risk allocation; add-on and compounding permission; factor duplication; opportunity cost versus other portfolio candidates; and post-trade attribution.

## Asset-native research stacks
### FX
Policy-rate path, yield and real-yield differentials, central-bank reaction functions, inflation/growth/employment surprise, sovereign risk, risk sentiment, cross-pair confirmation, positioning/flows, options volatility/skew, session liquidity, funding/carry, and technical/liquidity structure.

### Gold and silver
US and global real yields, USD regime, Federal Reserve path, inflation expectations, central-bank reserve demand, ETF flows, futures positioning and open interest, physical demand, geopolitical/safe-haven demand, options volatility/skew, COMEX/London session structure, liquidity, cross-confirmation with rates/USD/miners/commodities, and higher-timeframe supply/demand. XAUUSD must be researched every cycle even when no trade is opened.

### Crypto
Spot/perpetual basis, funding, open interest, liquidation structure, cross-venue order flow and liquidity, volatility, ETF/fund flows where relevant, stablecoin/liquidity conditions, market dominance/rotation, macro/rates/risk regime, catalyst risk, and technical/liquidity structure.

### Equity indices
Rates and discount-rate regime, earnings and revisions, breadth, sector/factor rotation, index concentration, futures basis, options implied volatility/skew/term structure and dealer positioning where obtainable, macro surprise, cross-index confirmation, session/open/close structure, and liquidity.

### Energy
Inventory and stock changes, futures curve/term structure, OPEC+ policy, production/refinery utilisation, shipping/logistics, weather, geopolitical disruption, USD/rates/macro demand regime, positioning, volatility, and session/liquidity structure.

### Deriv synthetic/volatility indices
Do not import real-world macro/news causality. Research must be native to the proprietary market: realised path statistics, volatility state, jump/step characteristics, serial/conditional structure where empirically demonstrated, time/tick behaviour, spread/contract/execution state and robust out-of-sample technical/path evidence. External news correlation must be treated as non-causal unless independently proven.

## Future stocks/futures expansion
If individual equities, listed futures or additional portfolios are later approved, they must receive their own native research stack before inclusion. They may not inherit FX, index or CFD research assumptions by convenience.

## Precision and loss-learning rule
Trades that briefly move favourable and then stop out are not automatically classified as unavoidable noise. They must be attributed to thesis error, regime error, catalyst error, timing/entry error, liquidity/execution error, stop calibration, factor duplication or economically unavoidable randomness. Avoidable classes become testable repair hypotheses.

## Flat-trade rule
Protected flat trades are capital-preservation outcomes, not automatically failures. However persistent high flat frequency must be decomposed into: correct thesis with premature protection; weak follow-through; poor entry location; missed partial-profit conversion; regime transition; liquidity/session mismatch; or economically correct scratch. Improvements must increase net captured P&L without converting protected flats into larger loss tails.

## Evidence discipline
New evidence families are added one at a time or through explicitly identified interactions. More data is not presumed to improve decisions. Each factor must prove incremental causal value through rolling-origin/out-of-sample testing under realistic costs, slippage, contract constraints and portfolio competition. Failed intelligence integrations remain evidence of what not to promote.

## Broker/execution rule
Research ranking and strategy selection are separate from broker/account routing. The selected opportunity must then be routed to the best certified broker/account profile for that route, session and regime using observed spread, commission, slippage, fill rate, rejection, latency, financing, contract size, minimum lot, lot step and margin. Introducing Broker remuneration may never override customer execution quality.
